# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First version: per-town bank accounts (cash and framework gold), gold bar investments, rented vault boxes, banker
  job (vault, loans with signature and collateral, telegraph transfers, gold exchange, appraisal, salaries, automatic
  mode), bills between players (`/billing`, `/mybills`) with fks-companies integration, government bill management
  and account freezes, Discord webhooks, server exports. English and Portuguese.
- Its own menu icons in `img/icons/*.svg` (deposit, vault, telegraph, loan, bill...), drawn by fks-core in the theme
  colours.
- Needs fks-core 2.2.0 (`table`, `signature` and `dropdown` components, icons as SVG markup, `Bridge.StashUsage`).
